# Privacy Policy

**PolyBatch does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.** The extension has no backend server. Nothing you do is reported anywhere.
- **No keys, no funds.** PolyBatch never accesses, requests, or stores your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet credentials. It does not move funds. Every order is signed by the wallet **you** have connected to Polymarket, exactly as if you clicked the buttons yourself.
- **Local only.** Your settings (selected options, share size, language) stay in your browser's local storage on your device.
- **Network requests.** The extension reads public Polymarket market data from `gamma-api.polymarket.com` and `clob.polymarket.com` to show live prices. No user data is sent in these requests.
- **Donation page.** The bundled donation page shows static crypto addresses and QR codes. It makes no network calls and sends nothing.

PolyBatch is free and open source — you can read every line of code in this repository to verify all of the above.
